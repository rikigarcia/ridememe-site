Privacy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

RideMeme is built privacy-first. We don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t build advertising profiles about you.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand overall traffic — things like total page views, the most-read sections, and country-level breakdown, so we know what’s worth writing more of. Like most websites, this involves standard analytics cookies and basic technical details such as browser type and general location, but we never combine this with anything that identifies you personally, and we don’t use it for advertising or cross-site tracking. See Google’s privacy policy for details on how Google handles this data.

Other third-party services

Our advertiser inquiry form is handled by Web3Forms, which relays submissions to us by email. Our newsletter signup is handled by Buttondown, which stores subscriber email addresses to send the digest. Neither service is used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

What we don’t do

We do not collect personal information, we do not sell or share data, and we do not run cross-site tracking.

Newsletter

If you choose to subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is used only to send the digest, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Links to other sites

Headlines and snippets link to the original publishers. Once you click through, you’re on their site, governed by their own privacy policies — not this one.

Questions

Reach us via the contact page.
